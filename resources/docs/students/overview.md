# Students overview

The Students module is the primary place to view and manage learner records for the active school.

## What a student record includes

A student record typically covers:

- Identity and profile details
- Enrolment and class placement
- Guardians and contacts
- Academic and attendance history (where enabled for your school)

Exact fields depend on your school’s configuration and the permissions granted to your account.

## Finding students

Use the student list (or grid) screens to filter by class, status, and other attributes supported by your school. Open a row to view the full student profile.

For day-to-day viewing guidance, see [Viewing a student](viewing-a-student).

:::can ability="student.view"
Administrators with **View Student** access can open individual profiles and related academic detail. The restricted [Student administration notes](administration) page covers additional controls available to staff with student permissions.
:::

:::cannot ability="student.view"
If you cannot open student profiles, ask a school administrator to grant the appropriate student viewing permission for your role.
:::
