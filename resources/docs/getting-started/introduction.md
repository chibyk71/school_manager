# Introduction

School Manager documentation lives inside the application. You must be signed in to read it. There is no separate documentation account.

## What you will find

| Area | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Getting started | Orientation and conventions |
| Students | Student records, lists, and related workflows |

## Visibility

Most pages are available to every authenticated user. A few pages that describe sensitive administrative workflows are limited to users who hold the corresponding School Manager permission (for example `student.view`).

Restricted pages are hidden from navigation and search when you do not have access. That is intentional: documentation must not leak material you are not authorised to see in the product itself.

## Feedback and improvement

Page views and searches are recorded in a privacy-conscious way (no user IDs or raw personal data retained longer than the configured window). Use these signals and your school’s normal support channels to improve the guides over time.
